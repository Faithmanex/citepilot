## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to site operators.

Cookies can be "first-party" (set by the website you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a service used by that website). Cookies can also be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (remaining on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) for lightweight, non-personal preferences. References to "cookies" in this policy include such technologies where relevant.

## 2. Cookie Consent

The Service sets no cookies that process personal data or track you. Because strictly necessary and preference cookies do not require consent, you will generally not see an intrusive consent banner. If we ever introduce analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies, we will:

- Update this policy first,
- Ask for your explicit consent before any non-essential cookie is set, and
- Store your choice in a first-party consent cookie (`cp_consent`) so we can respect it across visits.

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser (see §5.2) without losing core functionality.

## 3. Cookie Categories

### 3.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Service to function or to respect your stated choices. They do not require consent.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|-------------|----------|---------|------|----------|
| `cp_consent` | citepilot.com | Stores your cookie consent choice so we can respect it across visits (set only if a consent choice is offered/made). | First-party | 1 year |

There are **no authentication, session, or CSRF cookies**: the Service is anonymous and maintains no server-side sessions, so none are needed.

### 3.2. Functional Preferences (localStorage)

The Service remembers a small number of interface preferences using browser **local storage** rather than cookies. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

| Key | Storage Type | Purpose | Duration |
|-----|-------------|---------|----------|
| `cp_style_pref` | localStorage | Remembers your preferred citation style (e.g., APA 7, Harvard) so it is pre-selected on your next visit. | Until cleared |
| `cp_view_pref` | localStorage | Remembers your preferred results view mode (single-column, split-window, annotated). | Until cleared |
| `cp_theme` | localStorage | Stores your interface theme preference (light, dark, system). | Until cleared |
| `cp_filter_state` | localStorage | Remembers your last-used filter settings in the results view (e.g., "show only issues," "filter by author"). | Until cleared |

### 3.3. Analytics Cookies

**None.** We do not use web analytics tools, A/B testing platforms, or session-recording services, and we do not collect "pages visited" data. If we introduce privacy-preserving analytics in the future, it will be consent-gated and this policy will be updated first.

### 3.4. Marketing Cookies

**None.** We do not run third-party advertising on the Service and do not participate in ad networks or retargeting platforms. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any marketing cookies are set.

### 4.2. Other Providers

Our other service providers — Google (Gemini API), Crossref, DOI.org, OpenAlex, and PubMed — are contacted server-side via API and do not set cookies in your browser. ## 5. How to Manage Cookies

### 5.1. Through the Service

Because there are no accounts and no analytics cookies, there is nothing to configure in-app. Block or delete cookies in your browser if you prefer; the Service works without cookies (preferences simply reset to defaults).

### 5.2. Through Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Below are links to cookie management instructions for common browsers:

| Browser | Instructions |
|---------|-------------|
| Google Chrome | chrome://settings/cookies — or Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies |
| Mozilla Firefox | about:preferences#privacy — or Settings → Privacy & Security |
| Safari | Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data |
| Microsoft Edge | edge://settings/content/cookies — or Settings → Cookies and site permissions |
| Brave | brave://settings/cookies — or Settings → Shields → Cookies |

### 5.3. Impact of Disabling Cookies

| Cookie Category | Impact of Disabling |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Strictly necessary (consent preference) | The Service still works fully; a consent prompt (if any) may reappear on each visit. |
| Functional (localStorage preferences) | Your preferences (citation style, view mode, theme) will not be remembered between visits. You will need to re-select them each time. |
| Analytics / Marketing | No impact — none are in use. |

### 5.4. Do Not Track (DNT)

We do not use tracking or analytics cookies, so we have no behaviour to change in response to Do Not Track (DNT) signals. If we introduce consent-based analytics in the future, we will treat a DNT signal as a refusal and set no analytics cookies.

## 6. Local Storage and Session Storage

Beyond the preference keys listed in §3.2, the Service may use session storage only to hold an in-progress upload's metadata while you are on the page (cleared when the tab closes). No document content is written to storage in a retrievable form, and nothing in local/session storage is transmitted to our servers for advertising or analytics.
